# Bibit

> Bibit is an Indonesian retail investment application that lets users invest in mutual funds (reksa dana), government bonds (SBN/SBR/ORI), fixed-rate bonds, and stocks. It targets beginner investors with robo-advisor recommendations and is licensed by Indonesia's financial regulator OJK. Bibit is operated within the Stockbit group and is a portfolio company of Prosus Ventures. No public developer API or developer portal is published as of this writing.
